About Me

I'm sparkles ✨ — I train image and video super-resolution models and work on Remix Plus, my community fork of NVIDIA's RTX Remix. Everything I release is trained on custom, ethically sourced datasets. No scraping, no stolen art.

My models power upscaling workflows in tools across the community, and my work on Remix Plus brings path-traced skies, clouds, and new rendering features to classic games.

Remix Plus / Numos

Remix Plus is a community-maintained fork of NVIDIA's dxvk-remix that I lead. It extends the Remix SDK and adds new rendering features — including Numos, my custom atmosphere system with physically-based sky scattering, volumetric clouds, and a procedural night sky.
